There is definitely duplication in the codebase due to, in part, breaking down the framework/simulation into pieces smaller than being connected enough to exchange information as intended,
and making ways for different pieces to access or recreate needed info to the extent needed to pass a test. I've worked towards retroactively consolidating logic, but there are still
places to keep an eye out for duplicated logic, the one's I've noted off the bat are:
- DEBUG_MODE is present in, at the very least, one more place than needed, and probably more. I kept adding and integrating DEBUG_MODE constants for file-scoped testing/debugging, but the
            integration of it across the codebase is sloppy. It works as intended, just can be tweaked to be less... dumb.
- Most cleanup, either port, process, or file, is duplicated in some way or another, generally in separate parts of codebase. Doesn't cause issues (at least currently) but certainly reduces performance
- The different functions for running SUMO are unneeded in the grand scheme,  specific tests use different run/sumo functions, so to keep all the tests passing, they are still in place. Refactoring into 
            all of the tests using one function is a good move at some point
